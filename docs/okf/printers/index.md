# Printers

* [FDM machines](fdm.md) - Built-in cartesian printers are the Lime 220, Lime 300, and Lime 180, with PLA, PETG, ABS, and TPU filaments.
* [Belt machines](belt.md) - A belt request lays the part flat, slices it with the planar pipeline, and maps moves back into the gantry frame.
* [Resin and Form 4](resin-form-4.md) - The Formlabs Form 4 is an export-only resin target for split to print. This app does not slice resin.
